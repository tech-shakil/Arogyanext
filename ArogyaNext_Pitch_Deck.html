Proprietary Innovations & IP Strengths

System Flow & Architecture

Synchronized Multi-Tenant Cloud Architecture

Cloud Firestore real-time snapshot listeners ensure sub-50ms synchronization across all facility touchpoints.

01

Patient Check-In

Patient books via Android app (voice/OCR/body map) or front-desk receptionist. Live digital token (e.g. S-24) is issued with estimated arrival time.

02

Queue & TV Sync

Waiting Room TV updates in real-time. Patient receives countdown proximity alerts on their smartphone when approaching consultation.

03

Doctor Consultation

Doctor calls patient; TV announces token aloud. Doctor reviews ML Kit scanned past Rx, writes e-prescription, and e-signs on tablet pad.

04

E-Pharmacy Packing

Prescription syncs to hospital pharmacy immediately. Medicine is bagged and ready before patient walks to the dispensary counter.
